# Journal writing preferences

Before drafting or editing public copy, read `docs/writing-style.md`. Eric supplied handwritten notes and asked that the journal follow their direct, practical structure.

Use clear topic headings, explanations of what something does, ordered steps when sequence matters, and concrete tests/results. Write personal posts in first person using confirmed facts. Keep grammar clean and adapt note-taking structure into readable prose. Keep the public author name Eric Reyes.

Apply this guide to posts, About and Home copy, descriptions, and supporting text. Keep interface labels short and familiar. Do not change the approved visual design merely to change writing style. Never copy private scan contents, credentials, or identifiers into the repository. This is project guidance, not a claim that account-wide ChatGPT memory was updated.
