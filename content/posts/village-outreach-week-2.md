This week, we worked on the first design document draft, the UX style tile, and project setup for Village Outreach. Our app is about neighbors helping neighbors. People would be able to ask for help, offer support, and find local resources.

## First design document draft

I helped put together the first draft of our Software Design Document (SDD). It covers the app’s features, how people would use them, and our plans for the design, database, and testing. I also added Haley’s branding ideas to the draft.

I went through the draft and made the wording easier to follow. Some sections needed shorter explanations, and a few decisions still needed team review. I left those marked TBD. The point was to get our ideas together in a draft that everyone could read and help update.

## UX style tile

A style tile shows the colors, fonts, icons, and other visual choices for the app. We already had a direction, but I wanted to organize it better and include the missing UI examples.

I added a **Refined Style Tile** page in Figma while keeping the original. I grouped the content into six sections and added examples of text, buttons, forms, and feedback messages. I also replaced the image-based icons with editable vector icons so they would stay clear when resized.

## Review and project setup

I checked the layout and the six rubric categories, then shared the page in Discord. Matthew and Haley both said it looked good.

Working on the tile helped me connect UI and UX. The colors and fonts are part of the interface, but we also need to show what happens when someone uses it. For example, the form shows a message when required information is missing. That gives us a specific example to refer to later.

Haley also set up the GitHub repository and shared the branching instructions. That was part of getting the project organized. We reviewed how we would keep our changes separate and bring them together through pull requests.

## Next week

Next week, we’ll work on the final design document, the first wireframes and prototype draft, the Jira board, and planning our first sprint. For Week 2, my main contribution was helping with the document draft and making the style tile easier for the team to use.
